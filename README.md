# Overview

## Main Concept

- Brewer Buds is a React-based web application where users design their own custom drink in a playful, dress-up game like, hand-illustrated virtual café. Users start with an empty cup and build a drink step by step: choosing a cup, a drink base (coffee, tea, water or smoothie) with a milk option, syrups, and toppings! When the user is ready to hit “Brew!”, the app produces a final image of their drink along with an itemized receipt of everything they used. The user can then name their creation and add it to the site’s “menu”, aka the gallery display.

- Brewer Buds is more of a creative tool and outlet rather than a problem-solver. This application addresses the need for low-pressure and low-stimulant ways to explore drink combinations before ordering. Many people feel stuck choosing from long convoluted café menus or are unsure how flavors, bases and toppings will come together, and in ordering a custom drink can be intimidating or costly to get wrong. Brewer Buds let users experiment freely and just have fun! So, the next time they visit a coffee shop, they can feel more confident ordering what they want, how they want. As a creative outlet, I wanted to encapsulate early 2000s dress up games I loved playing as a kid and gives the users a place to design, name and save something they made as we live in a world where creativity can become a prompt line with little to no personality.

## Core Features

1. Cup Selector
2. Drink Base and Milk Selector
3. Syrup and Topping Customization
4. Live Cup Preview
5. “Brew!” Complete Button, Final Image, and Receipt
6. Drink Name and Gallery Display
